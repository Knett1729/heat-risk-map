# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Heat Risk Map to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback and differing viewpoints in good faith.
- Share only information you have permission to share. Do not post credentials, private data, or another person's personal information.
- Keep feedback relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the repository's GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and discreetly as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation in project spaces. They will consider the impact and context when deciding an appropriate response.
