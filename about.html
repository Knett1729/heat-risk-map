About Heat Risk Map

Purpose, methodology, and important context for interpreting the forecast.

Purpose

Heat waves are one of the most dangerous weather hazards, and El Niño makes them worse. Many people, especially farmers, outdoor workers, students, and the elderly, don't get clear, local, easy-to-read heat warnings. Heat Risk Map turns open weather data into a simple colored map and place-status list anyone can understand.

How it works

We take hourly temperature and relative humidity forecasts from Open-Meteo, compute the heat index using the NWS Rothfusz formula, and classify it with PAGASA-style levels: Caution, Extreme Caution, Danger, and Extreme Danger.

Limitations

Town-level only

The weather data comes from models with a grid several kilometers wide, so it can't show differences between streets or barangays.

Forecasts, not measurements

Values are estimates and can differ from real conditions. Always follow official PAGASA advisories.
